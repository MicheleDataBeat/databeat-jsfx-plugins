## License text check

The two pools' root `LICENSE` files are **not byte-identical**, contrary to what this task's brief assumed ("they are identical, check"). `sgossner/VCSL`'s copy carries the full standard CC0 Legal Code, including the Creative Commons attorney-client disclaimer header that `sgossner/VSCO-2-CE`'s copy omits, and the two are word-wrapped differently. After stripping that header and normalising whitespace the operative legal text matches (closely but not exactly): both are the CC0 1.0 Universal Statement of Purpose through the four numbered operative sections (Waiver, Public License Fallback, Limitations, disclaimer). `Shiro/Licenses/CC0-1.0.txt` ships the fuller VCSL copy verbatim as the archived legal text for both pools; the per-pool copies are kept in the development inventory for direct comparison.
